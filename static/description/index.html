Invoice Cost & Margin (Bill of Materials)

See what you really earn on every customer invoice. Manufactured products are costed from their bill of materials, on all levels.

The problem

Odoo shows a margin on sales orders only, computed from the product Cost field - which for manufactured products is usually outdated. Invoices, partial deliveries, credit notes and extra costs such as freight or customs are not taken into account at all.

What you get

Bill of materials, all levels

Manufactured products are costed by exploding their bill of materials down to purchased components, operations included - same formula as Odoo's BoM Structure & Cost report.

Purchased products

Product Cost or the price of the last confirmed purchase order - per company, overridable per product.

Exceptions per product

Exclude a product (re-invoiced services), give it a fixed cost in any currency, or do not explode its bill of materials.

Extra costs

Add freight, customs or subcontracting to an invoice, each in its own currency. They are included in the margin.

Average margin per sales order

Down payments, partial invoices and credit notes of the same orders are combined into one weighted average margin.

A snapshot, not a moving target

Computed when the invoice is confirmed (or with a button) and stored, with who and when. Later price changes do not rewrite history.

How to use it

1
Go to Invoicing > Configuration > Settings, section Invoice Cost & Margin: compute on confirmation (on by default), include operations, cost of purchased products.
2
Optionally, on a product: tab Accounting, section Invoice Cost & Margin - exclude it, give it a fixed cost or keep its bill of materials closed.
3
Confirm a customer invoice. Open the tab Cost & Margin: products cost, extra costs, margin and margin %, cost of each line and where it comes from.
4
Add Extra Costs on that tab if needed; the margin is updated at once. The invoice list has optional Margin columns for totals.

Good to know

  • Cost and margin are visible to Billing Administrators and to users given the group "Show Invoice Cost & Margin".
  • Older invoices: enable the scheduled action "Invoice Cost & Margin: compute older invoices" (Settings > Technical > Scheduled Actions) or use the filter "Cost Not Computed".
  • Recursive bills of materials are detected; products without cost are listed in the warnings of the invoice.
  • Kits are exploded like any other bill of materials.
